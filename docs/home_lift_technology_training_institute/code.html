The LIFT Approach

Learn by doing.
Grow by applying.

Technology skills become valuable when you can use them. LIFT’s learning approach emphasizes guided exercises, relevant examples, reflection and practical outputs.

Participants are encouraged to understand what AI can do, recognize its limits and practice using it responsibly in their own contexts.
