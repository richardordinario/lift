Frequently Asked Questions

Key operational guidelines for institutional leads and department chairs.

Can training take place at our corporate or school premises?

Yes. Our trainers can travel to your campus or office equipped with localized lab configurations, provided basic internet access and terminal devices are available.

Are the programs eligible for DepEd / CHED / CSR credits?

Through the St. Mutien College academic framework and PCI Tech Center, programs include formal certificates of completion with verified clock hours and competency outcomes.

What is the optimal cohort size for interactive sessions?

To ensure direct facilitator engagement during hands-on lab projects, we recommend cohorts of 20 to 35 participants per room, with multi-batch rotation for large organizations.

Can we customize curriculum modules around proprietary software?

Yes. During Step 2 (Collaborative Planning), our instructional team can audit your specific toolstack and incorporate direct workplace tasks into the training syllabus.
