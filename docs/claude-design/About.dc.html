About LIFT

Technology education grounded in practical learning.

The Learning Institute for Future Technologies (LIFT) is a Technology Training Institute formed through the joint venture of PCI Tech Center Inc. and St. Mutien College. We connect emerging technologies with the needs of learners, teachers, professionals and organizations.

Our story

Established in 2025.

LIFT was established in 2025 to make future-oriented technology education more accessible and useful. Starting from a campus-based training environment and an applied technology curriculum, LIFT has developed hands-on learning experiences and collaborative programs that connect academic learning with practical skills.

Our purpose

We help people understand, use and adapt to technologies that are changing the way we teach, work and create. Our present focus is artificial intelligence education for three communities: educators, the BPO workforce, and youth and young professionals.

How we work

We believe strong technology education includes more than demonstrations. Effective learning makes room for practice, discussion, mistakes, feedback and useful outputs. We design toward real situations while keeping ethical and responsible use at the center of the learning experience.
