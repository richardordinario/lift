FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is LIFT?

LIFT is a Technology Training Institute formed through the joint venture of PCI Tech Center Inc. and St. Mutien College.

Who can join?

Our current AI education programs are designed for teachers, BPO-related learners and professionals, and youth and young professionals. Specific eligibility depends on the program and cohort.

Where is training held?

LIFT has a campus-based training presence in Pampanga. The available delivery mode for each program may vary; see the program page or ask the team.

Will I receive a certificate?

Completion documentation, if offered, will be explained on the relevant program page or by the program coordinator. It should not be confused with external licensure or professional accreditation.

Can an organization book a training cohort?

Yes. Schools, employers, foundations and other organizations may contact LIFT to discuss a group program and suitable delivery arrangement.

What are the fees and schedules?

Fees, schedules and enrollment conditions are published or communicated for each approved cohort. Contact LIFT for the latest confirmed details.

Do participants need prior AI experience?

This depends on the specific course. LIFT can advise on prerequisites and the right pathway for your starting point.
