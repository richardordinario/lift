## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-thinking, pragmatic academic posture. Engineered for modern AI education across teachers, workforce professionals, and youth, the visual language balances institutional credibility with technical vitality. It strips away speculative technology clichés—rejecting holographic tropes and abstract android imagery—in favor of clean modular layouts, authentic classroom imagery, and structural clarity.

The aesthetic fuses functional minimalism with crisp corporate precision:
- **Tone:** Academic rigor meets agile technical capability. Grounded, inspiring, and structurally deliberate.
- **Canvas Atmosphere:** Clean, uncluttered white foundations with calculated negative space to reduce cognitive fatigue during complex curriculum delivery.
- **Graphic Elements:** Precise upward-directed motifs, ascending geometric cube accents, and dual-tone partnership indicators reflecting steady professional growth.

## Colors

The palette balances deep authoritative anchors with sharp kinetic accents. Core structural tones govern roughly 70% of surfaces, while chromatic green and blue accents occupy 20% to 30% for interactive milestones and focus markers.

### Contrast & Legibility Rules
- **LIFT Green (`#7CC144`) Constraint:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) on LIFT Green fails WCAG standards at 2.19:1. White typography on LIFT Green backgrounds is strictly forbidden. All labels, glyphs, or iconography situated over LIFT Green must render in Deep Navy (`#0D1C60`), delivering a compliant 7.08:1 contrast ratio.
- **LIFT Blue (`#2457D5`):** Serves as the primary operational CTA token. Text placed on LIFT Blue must be pure white (`#FFFFFF`), achieving 6.18:1 contrast.
- **Citrus Yellow (`#FFD54F`):** Dedicated exclusively to campaign alerts and positive callouts. Text placed on Citrus Yellow must render in Deep Navy (`#0D1C60`) at 11.01:1 contrast.
- **Body & Structural Copy:** Standard long-form reading copy uses Deep Navy (`#0D1C60`) on clean light surfaces. Supporting annotations and secondary meta copy transition to Charcoal (`#333333`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy communicates institutional precision through geometric titling paired with accessible, humanized body prose.

- **Primary Display & Headings:** Set in Montserrat (Bold and SemiBold). These structural anchors establish strong editorial weight for hero displays, curriculum milestones, and major section transitions.
- **Body & Extended Prose:** Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans. Its open apertures, geometric balance, and clean legibility ensure sustained comfort across technical lesson material and administrative dashboards.
- **Interactive Labels & Navigation:** Set in Montserrat (Medium and SemiBold) to preserve crisp legibility across buttons, tabs, data points, and status tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a deliberate 12-column modular grid anchored by proportional base-8 spacing increments. Content breathes within generous margins to maintain an academic editorial standard.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid structure within a maximum container width of `1280px`. Gutters remain steady at `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer canvas padding set to `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Gutters compress to `1.25rem` (`20px`) with `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Two-column card modules collapse or scale to 4 columns.
- **Mobile (360px – 767px):** 4-column stacking system. Gutters adjust to `1rem` (`16px`) with outer canvas margins of `1.25rem` (`20px`). Interactive targets maintain minimum touch zones of `44px`. Horizontal canvas overflow is strictly disallowed.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids skeuomorphic drops, fuzzy ambient shadows, and heavy layered elevations in favor of clean planar alignment and tonal containment.

- **Surface Layering:** Depth is communicated through color plane differentiation. The core canvas relies on pure white (`#FFFFFF`), while nested cards, curriculum trays, and contextual panels shift to muted gray (`#E6E9ED`) or crisp 1px borders using `#B0B3B8`.
- **Card Hover & Focus Elevation:** In place of heavy drop shadows, interactive card surfaces elevate via a crisp `1px` structural outline shift from `#B0B3B8` to LIFT Blue (`#2457D5`), paired with a micro-translation of `-2px` along the Y-axis.
- **Focus Indicators:** Interactive components invoke an explicit focus treatment consisting of a `2px` solid ring in Deep Navy (`#0D1C60`) set with a `2px` offset.
- **Logo Treatment:** Shadows, artificial ambient glows, or gradient underlays beneath institutional insignia are strictly prohibited.

## Shapes

The interface implements a restrained soft-corner profile (`roundedness: 1`). This standardizes interactive perimeters to `0.25rem` (`4px`) on default surfaces, scaling to `0.5rem` (`8px`) on larger institutional cards and dialogs.

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Controls):** Radius is locked to `0.25rem` (`4px`), providing a disciplined, architectural profile that avoids informal pill styling while softening harsh corners.
- **Modular Cards & Modules:** Bound by `0.5rem` (`8px`) corners to delineate course modules, track summaries, and dashboard panels cleanly.
- **Icon Treatment:** Icons observe uniform stroke geometry with soft internal joints and rounded caps, constructed with consistent 1.5px to 2px line weights.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (LIFT Blue):** Solid fill `#2457D5` with `#FFFFFF` text (Montserrat SemiBold). Height: `44px` minimum. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Corner radius: `0.25rem`. Hover state: Darken fill by 6%.
- **Secondary Action (LIFT Green):** Solid fill `#7CC144` with `#0D1C60` text (Montserrat SemiBold). Height: `44px` minimum. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Corner radius: `0.25rem`. Hover state: Darken fill by 6%.
- **Outline Action:** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#2457D5` and `#2457D5` text. On dark navy sections, outline shifts to `#FFFFFF` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Program Chips:** Compact containers with `0.25rem` radius and `0.25rem 0.625rem` internal padding.
- **Technology/AI Tags:** Background `#E6E9ED`, text `#0D1C60` (Montserrat SemiBold, `0.75rem`).
- **Active Cohort Badge:** Background `#7CC144`, text `#0D1C60`.
- **Institutional Track Badge:** Background `#097934`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Course & Content Cards
- **Base Surface:** Flat `#FFFFFF` canvas, `0.5rem` corner radius, encased by a solid `1px` border in `#B0B3B8`.
- **Inner Padding:** `1.5rem` evenly distributed.
- **Header Section:** Montserrat SemiBold title in `#0D1C60` above a short description in Charcoal (`#333333`).
- **Footer Section:** Border-top divider in `#E6E9ED`, housing progress indicators and a secondary CTA button.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Input:** Border: `1px` solid `#B0B3B8`; background: `#FFFFFF`; corner radius: `0.25rem`; height: `44px`; padding: `0 1rem`. Text: `#0D1C60`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts directly to `#2457D5` accompanied by the global `2px` Deep Navy (`#0D1C60`) offset focus ring.
- **Labeling:** Montserrat SemiBold (`0.875rem`), positioned `0.375rem` above the field in Deep Navy (`#0D1C60`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** Flat white box with `1.5px` outline in `#B0B3B8`. Checkbox radius: `2px`; radio: circular.
- **Checked:** Solid `#2457D5` fill displaying a pure `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip.
- **Focus Indicator:** Standard `2px` offset ring in `#0D1C60`.

### Brand Accents & Motifs
- **Ascending Geometric Accent:** Standardized 45-degree angled chevron or segmented upward cubes rendered exclusively in `#7CC144` or `#2457D5` to signify upward trajectory. Used sparingly on section headers and hero edges.
- **Institutional Partnership Divider:** Dual-tone horizontal hairline divider featuring `#2457D5` along the initial 30% and `#7CC144` along the remaining 70%.
