Program Inquiries & Enrollment Clarity

Key operational specifics about LIFT training programs.

LIFT (Learning Institute for Future Technologies) is a premier AI education joint venture between PCI Tech Center and St. Mutien College. We provide practical, ethics-centered AI curriculum designed to empower educators, business professionals, and emerging students.
Our programs are tiered to welcome diverse backgrounds. Track 1 is tailored for K-12 and tertiary educators; Track 2 supports BPO, frontline support, and IT-BPM professionals; Track 3 is engineered for collegiate students and early career starters. We welcome both individual enrollments and enterprise cohorts.
Instruction occurs at our specialized training center in Dau, Mabalacat, Pampanga. Additionally, hybrid and remote-synchronous options are scheduled regularly to accommodate national and international learners.
Yes. All learners who fulfill core coursework, demonstrate practical workflow mastery, and complete the final hands-on capstone project receive a verifiable Certificate of Completion jointly ratified by PCI Tech Center and St. Mutien College.
No technical, programming, or computer science background is required. The curriculum emphasizes cognitive workflows, responsible prompting, contextual critical thinking, and operational efficiency rather than complex algorithm development.
