## 1 Brand positioning and market promise

### 1.1 One sentence identity

LIFT is a Technology Training Institute that makes artificial intelligence and emerging digital technologies practical for educators, BPO talent, young people and organizations.

### 1.2 Core positioning statement

For Filipinos and institutions preparing for technology-driven work and learning, LIFT offers accessible, applied training in emerging technologies. Through the academic strengths of St. Mutien College and the industry-facing experience of PCI Tech Center Inc., LIFT turns technology awareness into guided practice, useful outputs and adaptable skills.

### 1.3 Proposed website message

Learn new technology. Apply it with confidence. This is proposed campaign language, not a formally approved LIFT tagline. The supplied brand guide leaves its Tagline field unfilled; do not present any new line as the official institutional slogan without approval.

### 1.4 Positioning hierarchy

|**Level**|**Website expression**|**Why it matters**|
|-|-|-|
|Category|Technology Training Institute|An immediately understandable institution type.|
|Present specialty|Practical AI education|Clear emphasis for the current course portfolio.|
|Audience|Teachers; BPO talent; youth and young professionals|Visitors can recognize their path.|
|Method|Guided, applied and responsible learning|Explains why LIFT is different.|
|Enduring identity|A joint venture of PCI and STM|Makes the partnership explicit and credible.|

### 1.5 Official brand personality and messaging pillars

The supplied guide defines LIFT as modern, visionary and academic, combining technological confidence with educational warmth. The required voice is inspiring, professional, inclusive and forward-thinking. The official values are Innovation, Integrity, Collaboration, Excellence and Sustainability.

Its four messaging pillars are Innovation in Education, Technology for Growth, Partnership for Excellence and Learning for the Future. Use those pillars as the editorial test for website headlines, descriptions and campaign materials.

* Practical: describe what learners will do, not only what they will know.
* Forward-looking: connect today’s training to new work, learning and opportunities.
* Responsible: emphasize safe, thoughtful and ethical use of AI.
* Encouraging: make technical subjects welcoming to people with different starting points.
* Credible: use documented evidence, exact partnership names and honest claims.

**Do say.** “Hands-on AI learning,” “applied exercises,” “designed for educators,” “for aspiring and current BPO talent,” “skills for the next stage of work.”

**Avoid.** “Guaranteed employment,” “government accredited,” “official CPD units,” “recognized certification,” “industry-leading,” or “master AI in days” unless independently verified.

## 2 Visual identity and brand system

### 2.1 Official logo and partnership signature

The official LIFT mark depicts ascending geometric cubes and an upward arrow. Use the exact brand-provided full-color green emblem as the primary version; black and white are the allowed monochrome variants. The supplied brand guide illustrates the authentic full-color mark. For production, request the approved vector or transparent master file.

**Official logo asset:** The full-color LIFT cube-and-arrow emblem is included in the Word version and in the supplied *LIFT Brand Guide 2025* (page 5). Use the official master logo asset when building the website. This Markdown version intentionally keeps the image as a textual reference so the file is self-contained and has no broken media links.

Required official partnership signature: “A Joint Venture of PCI Tech Center Inc. and St. Mutien College.” Use it on official website materials, including the About page and footer. The source guide elsewhere spells the company “PCI Innovations Tech Center, Inc.”; confirm exact public/legal naming before publishing. Partner logos should be equal in size and given consistent spacing when all three logos are shown.

* Primary site header: official full-color LIFT mark with an approved institutional wordmark/lockup if supplied. Do not typeset an improvised logo.
* Secondary use: official black mark on light backgrounds and white mark on dark backgrounds; no unofficial recoloring.
* Clear space: at least the height of the letter “L” in LIFT around the mark, free of text and graphic elements.
* Minimum size: digital logo height 100 px; print logo height 1 inch (2.54 cm). For a smaller favicon, confirm a dedicated icon variant.
* Never distort, rotate, add shadows or outlines, alter proportions or place the mark on textured/gradient backgrounds.

#### 2.2.1 Color use and contrast

Brand guidance: core colors should provide roughly 70% of the visual identity; expressive colors occupy about 20–30% as accents, while neutral backgrounds preserve clarity. Recommended pairings include LIFT Blue + Sky Blue for technology themes; LIFT Green + Citrus Yellow for education campaigns; Deep Navy + Light Gray for formal/academic uses; and Emerald Teal + LIFT Blue for co-branding.

Accessibility matters: do not set white text on LIFT Green (#7CC144), which has only about 2.19:1 contrast. Use Deep Navy text on LIFT Green (about 7.08:1), white text on LIFT Blue (about 6.18:1), or Deep Navy text on Citrus Yellow (about 11.01:1). Verify contrast of every final image overlay, link and focus state.

### 2.4 Visual mood

Modern academic credibility meets optimistic technology learning. Use authentic images of instructors facilitating sessions, teachers designing digital learning materials, young adults collaborating and BPO learners practicing service workflows. Favor daylight, uncluttered labs, inclusive Filipino classroom and workplace contexts, and candid human expressions. Avoid artificial “futuristic city” clichés, exaggerated holograms or fabricated event photos.

### 2.5 Graphic elements and imagery standards

* Use geometric elements based on ascending cubes and an upward direction to express structured progression.
* Use curved green-blue bars to suggest partnership and movement, while keeping the official logo untouched.
* Use minimal flat icons with rounded corners, consistent line weights and outlines in LIFT Green or LIFT Blue.
* Photos should feature actual students, faculty and collaborators in natural light. Obtain permission for recognizable subjects, especially minors.
* The source guide mentions subtle gradient graphic effects but explicitly prohibits the logo on gradient backgrounds. Prefer solid core tones for web UI; seek approval for any decorative gradients.

### 2.7 Web interface design recipe

Use white as the primary page background. A slim light-gray navigation border, LIFT Green highlights and restrained LIFT Blue links give the interface an academic, future-oriented character. The hero can pair a concise Montserrat headline and two clear actions with a licensed, authentic training photo. Place three audience-specific program cards immediately below, followed by methodology, partnership, documented impact and an inquiry section.

Suggested tokens for a design tool: primary-green #7CC144; primary-blue #2457D5 (pending confirmation); institutional-green #097934; surface #FFFFFF; surface-muted #E6E9ED; text #0D1C60; text-muted #333333; border #B0B3B8; CTA-primary #2457D5 with white text; CTA-secondary #7CC144 with Deep Navy text; focus-ring #0D1C60. These tokens are a website implementation mapping of the official palette, not a replacement for the brand guide.
